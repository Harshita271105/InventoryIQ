   BUSINESS LOGIC

1. Purchase
   stock = stock + quantity

2. Sale
   stock = stock - quantity
   Do not allow negative stock

3. Return
   stock = stock + quantity

4. Adjustment
   stock changes according to adjustment

5. Stock Status
   0 = OUT OF STOCK
   <= 50% reorder level = CRITICAL
   <= reorder level = LOW STOCK
   otherwise = HEALTHY

6. Inventory Value
   stock × unit price

7. Transaction Total
   quantity × unit price

8. Average Daily Demand
   units sold / number of days

9. Days Until Stockout
   current stock / average daily demand

10. Fast Moving
    High sales volume during selected period

11. Slow Moving
    Low sales volume during selected period

12. Dead Stock
    No sales for 90+ days

13. Recommended Reorder Quantity
    Expected demand during lead time
    + safety stock
    - current stock

14. Inventory Health Score
    Based on stock availability,
    demand accuracy,
    turnover efficiency,
    and supplier reliability

15. ABC Analysis
    A = High Value
    B = Medium Value
    C = Low Value

16. Inventory Aging
    0–30 days
    31–60 days
    61–90 days
    91–180 days
    180+ days
